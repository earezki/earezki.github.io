Mastering CKAD: Certified Kubernetes Application Developer

Mastering CKAD: Certified Kubernetes Application Developer

A hands-on guide with Kind, real-world examples, and exam preparation — from core concepts to mock exams.

This book is structured around the official CKAD curriculum (v1.30+). Every chapter includes a local workspace powered by Kind (Kubernetes IN Docker), hands-on exercises with increasing difficulty, and scenario-based questions modeled on real exam tasks. The exam is 100% practical — you write manifests and run kubectl. No multiple choice. Train accordingly.

What You Will Master

  • Core Concepts (20%): Pods, Namespaces, Labels, Selectors, and the declarative model
  • Workloads & Scheduling (15%): Deployments, Jobs, CronJobs, init containers, multi-container patterns, taints, tolerations, and affinity
  • Services & Networking (20%): ClusterIP, NodePort, Ingress, NetworkPolicy, and DNS resolution
  • Storage (8%): Volumes, PersistentVolumeClaims, StorageClasses, and access modes
  • Configuration (18%): ConfigMaps, Secrets, SecurityContext, ServiceAccounts, RBAC, and ResourceQuotas
  • Observability (8%): Liveness/Readiness/Startup probes, logging, events, and ephemeral debug containers
  • Advanced Topics (11%): Helm, CRDs, StatefulSets, and DaemonSets
  • Exam Readiness: Speed techniques, imperative workflows, two full mock exams with detailed solutions
