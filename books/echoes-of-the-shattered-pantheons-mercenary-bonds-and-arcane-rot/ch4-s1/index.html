Echoes in the Void

Oakhaven succumbs to 34% structural decay from Arcane Rot. The Convergence group, formed by a Fallen Paladin, Technomantic Saboteur, Disgraced Archivist, and feral Kineticist, navigates the city's treacherous landscape to retrieve the Shattered Aegis.

Confronting the Shadows of Oakhaven

The Weight of Guilt and the Rot’s Influence

As the group delves deeper into the heart of Oakhaven, the shadows of their past begin to surface. Silas, the disgraced archivist and ducal heir, is haunted by the guilt of suppressing information that led to the initial Rot breach in the archives. The weight of his actions bears down on him, a constant reminder of the devastating consequences of his silence. Mira, the technomantic saboteur, is tormented by the knowledge that she sold experimental tech to the Iron Bank, which was used to enforce the first quarantine, condemning countless lives to the mercy of the Rot.

The city itself seems to be awakening, its ancient magic now a distorted, humming frequency that resonates through the crystalline structures that have replaced its solid foundations. The Rot, a magical contagion that transforms matter into porous, humming crystalline structures, has become a constant presence, its influence seeping into the very fabric of the city. The group must navigate this treacherous landscape, avoiding the Rot’s corrupting influence and the twisted creatures that now roam the streets.

Visions of the Past

As they journey deeper into the city, the group begins to experience visions of their own past, each one linked to the city’s Rot lore. Vex, the kineticist, is plagued by the memories of his blinks, which are powered by environmental Rot, not internal mana storage. The risk of lung-calcification, a terminal condition caused by inhaled Rot spores, hangs over him like a specter, a constant reminder of the dangers of his abilities. Kaelen, the fallen paladin, is haunted by the memories of his fractured blade, which draws power from localized mana rot, increasing the rate of user lung-calcification by 12%.

The visions are a manifestation of the city’s attempt to communicate with them, to convey the secrets of the Rot and the true nature of the Shattered Aegis. The group must decipher the meaning behind these visions, using their knowledge of the city’s lore and their own experiences to uncover the truth. The fate of Oakhaven hangs in the balance, and the group is the only one who can save it from the clutches of the Rot.

The Quest for the Shattered Aegis

The group’s quest for the Shattered Aegis, a legendary containment vessel and the only known artifact capable of isolating the Rot’s point of origin, becomes increasingly urgent. The Convergence, a merc group, has offered a reward of 50,000 Imperial Credits and quarantine passage to anyone who can retrieve the Aegis. The group must navigate the treacherous landscape of Oakhaven, avoiding the Rot’s corrupting influence and the twisted creatures that now roam the streets, to reach the Aegis before it falls into the wrong hands.

The journey will be fraught with danger, and the group will be forced to confront their own demons in order to succeed. The Rot’s influence will try to break them, to turn them against each other, but they must remain strong, using their unique skills and abilities to overcome the challenges that lie ahead. The fate of Oakhaven, and their own souls, depends on it.
