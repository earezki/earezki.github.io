Every ecommerce hero needs a Sidekick

Shopify unveiled Sidekick, its new AI assistant built to reason about commerce data, in January 2026. This agent is currently being used internally to refine Shopify’s product taxonomy, a key component of discoverability for millions of products.

Why This Matters

Ideal product categorization relies on nuanced understanding and constant adaptation, whereas traditional methods are often manual, slow, and prone to inconsistencies. A poorly maintained taxonomy impacts search relevance, decreasing conversion rates and ultimately costing merchants revenue; accurate categorization directly correlates to sales, with studies showing a 5-10% uplift with improved taxonomy accuracy.

Key Insights

  • Shopify’s AI focus: Announced ongoing AI product development in May 2025.
  • AI Agents for Taxonomy: Sidekick demonstrates how AI agents can automate complex data organization.
  • Commerce Specific AI: Focuses on building AI tools directly tied to the constraints and opportunities of ecommerce platforms.

Practical Applications

  • Use Case: Shopify utilizes Sidekick to dynamically update product categorization based on emerging trends and customer behavior.
  • Pitfall: Over-reliance on AI-generated tags without human review can lead to miscategorization and decreased search accuracy.
