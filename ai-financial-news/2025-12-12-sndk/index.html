Despite weak profitability and negative free cash flow, Sandisk is being repositioned in the market narrative as an enabler of AI infrastructure, benefiting from rising NAND pricing power and data center storage demand. With institutional ownership exceeding 43% and analysts maintaining a “Buy” consensus, sentiment is acting as a catalyst that may temporarily override fundamental weaknesses in the short term.
