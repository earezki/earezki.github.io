Ulta Beauty generated $964 million in free cash flow over the trailing twelve months while reporting a 12.9% year-over-year sales increase in Q3 2025. The stock surged nearly 6% in after-hours trading following the earnings release, signaling strong investor sentiment.

Why This Matters

Ulta’s robust free cash flow and healthy gross margin of 42.8% reflect operational efficiency and pricing power, which are being amplified by strategic wins in high-growth K-beauty segments. With same-store sales up 6.3% and exclusive partnerships driving traffic, the company is capitalizing on shifting consumer preferences—particularly among younger demographics—making this momentum highly relevant in the current holiday shopping season.
