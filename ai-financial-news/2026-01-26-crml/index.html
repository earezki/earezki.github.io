Why This Matters

Despite lacking profitability and free cash flow, Critical Metals Corp. is transitioning from pure exploration to near-term commercialization, as evidenced by the 50/50 JV with a Saudi industrial partner—providing off-take security and de-risking future revenue. With the Bankable Feasibility Study 70% complete and on track for late 2025 delivery, the market is now pricing in imminent project advancement, amplified by geopolitical tailwinds favoring Western-aligned critical mineral supply chains.
