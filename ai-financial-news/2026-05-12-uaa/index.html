UAA – Decrease Expected (5-day)

Under Armour, Inc. operates in the highly competitive consumer cyclical sector, specifically apparel manufacturing. The company develops, markets, and distributes performance apparel, footwear, and accessories globally. Recently, the brand has struggled with shifting consumer preferences, elevated inventory levels across the industry, and macroeconomic pressures, prompting a major restructuring effort under returning CEO Kevin Plank to streamline SKUs and cut costs.

Under Armour shares plummeted 14% premarket after reporting Q4 revenue of $1.17 billion, drastically missing the $1.68 billion consensus. This top-line collapse, paired with a 470 basis point gross margin contraction, highlights severe operational headwinds.

When a consumer cyclical brand misses revenue estimates by nearly 30% while simultaneously suffering severe margin compression from tariffs and product costs, it signals deep structural issues.
