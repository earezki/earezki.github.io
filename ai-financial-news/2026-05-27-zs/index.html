ZS – Trading Sideways Expected (21-day)

Zscaler, Inc. is a cloud security company providing zero trust architecture solutions globally. The company closed Q3 FY2026 with 25% ARR growth, record non-GAAP operating margins of 23%, and guided FY2026 EPS of $4.10-$4.11. However, FY2027 ARR and revenue growth are expected to decelerate to 16%-17%, signaling a growth moderation that investors must weigh against improving profitability.

Zscaler just posted 25% ARR growth and record margins — yet guided for 16% growth next year. The market cheered the beat, but the forward trajectory paints a starkly different picture.
