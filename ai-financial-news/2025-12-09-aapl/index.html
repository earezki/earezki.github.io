Apple generated $98.77 billion in trailing free cash flow, signaling robust operational strength despite a high debt/equity ratio. This financial resilience is being re-rated by analysts amid rising optimism around AI-driven Siri enhancements and recent tariff relief from EU regulatory progress.

With shares down 3.15% in the past week but still up 10.7% YTD, the pullback presents a tactical entry point ahead of potential AI-related catalysts and a seasonal strength period in December.
