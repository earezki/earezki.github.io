Structure Therapeutics reported a negative free cash flow of $117.9M over the trailing twelve months, reflecting its clinical-stage burn rate. Why This Matters

The biotech sector, especially obesity-focused companies, remains highly sensitive to clinical trial outcomes, and Structure Therapeutics’ oral GLP-1 candidate GSBR-1290 (aleniglipron) has demonstrated best-in-class weight loss potential without requiring injections—addressing a key market gap. With $915.3 million in cash as of Q3 2024 funding operations into 2027, the company has both the financial runway and scientific momentum to sustain investor enthusiasm in the near term, especially as it prepares for Phase 3 readiness.
