Fastly, Inc. operates an edge cloud platform providing infrastructure as a service (IaaS) to optimize the delivery and security of web and application traffic. Positioned in the highly competitive edge computing sector alongside Cloudflare and Akamai, Fastly leverages a traffic-metered business model that makes it uniquely sensitive to the volume of AI agent traffic.

Fastly shares spiked 15% today following an Oppenheimer upgrade to Outperform with a $35 price target. This momentum is coupled with the launch of three new AI-focused security products: AI Runtime, AI Firewall, and API Enforcement.

The shift toward agentic AI increases the volume of edge traffic, which directly benefits Fastly’s metered revenue model. The analyst upgrade validates the market’s belief that Fastly can successfully monetize AI traffic and cross-sell its new security suite to increase average deal sizes, potentially offsetting its current lack of profitability.

Given the immediate market reaction and the specific price target, this catalyst is capable of driving the stock toward the $35 level within a short window.
