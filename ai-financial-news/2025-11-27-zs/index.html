ZS – increase in Days/Weeks

Free cash flow surged to $726.7M (TTM) on 26% YoY revenue growth, yet the stock dropped 9.7% post-earnings due to mixed analyst reactions. The market overreacted to conservative near-term EPS guidance while underappreciating the strategic value of the SPLX acquisition and sustained top-line momentum.

Why This Matters

Zscaler’s core financials reflect strong demand for cloud-native zero trust security, with gross margin holding at 76.8% and operating leverage improving despite heavy investment (CapEx: $245.8M TTM). The post-earnings dip contradicts fundamentally positive signals — including a revenue guidance hike to $3.3B — suggesting irrational investor behavior typical in high-growth, high-P/E names after minor earnings misses in profitability expectations. With institutional ownership stable at 50.84% and key holders like Vanguard and BlackRock maintaining positions, the sell-off appears short-lived.

Key Insights

  • Revenue +26% YoY, Q1 2026: $788.1M actual vs. $773.75M estimate, signaling resilient enterprise spending on cybersecurity.
  • News Impact: Acquisition of AI security firm SPLX strengthens Zscaler’s Gen AI data protection suite, aligning with rising demand for secure Copilot and SaaS integrations.
  • Risk/Offset: High forward P/E of 70.8x and debt/equity of 92.65% amplify volatility, but strong FCF and no near-term liquidity risk support valuation.

Practical Implications

  • Bull Case: Reversion to growth premium as market digests guidance; potential 15–20% upside toward $290+ by mid-December on short-covering and institutional re-entry.
  • Bear Case: Continued profit-taking if broader tech multiples compress under rate pressure, risking near-term test of $230 support.
  • Confidence: 7/10 – Fundamentals and trajectory outweigh temporary sentiment drag; earnings momentum favors upside.
