DAN – Trading Sideways Expected (21-day)

Dana Incorporated is an auto parts supplier undergoing a fundamental transformation. It recently sold its Off-Highway business and announced a combination with Eaton’s Mobility Business to create a ~$11B sales leader in power-conveyance and energy-management. The new CEO, Byron Foster, takes over July 1. The stock has moved from a 52-week low of $15.31 to $30.11, but the RSI of 37.6 suggests a short-term oversold condition after a pullback from the high of $39.56.

When a stock has an RSI below 38, a forward P/E of 8.2, AND a 135% dividend yield, the market is pricing in serious distress – but the M&A and earnings news suggest a possible turnaround. Which narrative wins in the next three weeks?

Dana is a bellwether for the legacy auto parts sector pivoting to electrification. For traders, the next 21 days cover the Q1 filing grace period, the CEO transition, and initial shareholder reaction to the merger plan.

The market is right to be skeptical of margin expansion until proven.
