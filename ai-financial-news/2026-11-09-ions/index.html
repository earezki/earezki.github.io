Ionis Pharmaceuticals is a commercial-stage biotechnology company specializing in RNA-targeted medicines. Operating in the high-risk, high-reward biotech sector, the company manages a diverse pipeline including treatments for familial chylomicronemia syndrome (FCS) and hereditary angioedema, while facing significant financial pressure from high R&D costs and debt.

Ionis has slashed its 2026 Tryngolza sales forecast by approximately 52%, pushing its break-even milestone back to 2029. This guidance cut coincides with a stock price that has already plummeted 28% in a single day.
