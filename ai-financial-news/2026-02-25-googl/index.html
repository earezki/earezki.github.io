When a $3.79 trillion mega-cap stock drops into oversold territory (RSI 31.33) despite beating earnings and accelerating revenue by 15%, it creates a critical inflection point. The primary 21-day catalyst capable of driving a 5%+ move is the market’s digestion and repricing of the $240 billion Cloud backlog against the oversold technical setup. Furthermore, the rollout of the Samsung S26 featuring deep Gemini AI integration provides a tangible, near-term monetization pathway that can shift the narrative away from CapEx fears back to revenue generation.
