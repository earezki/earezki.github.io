Difference between 'Verification' and 'Validation'?

Click to reveal  ·  Space
Quick Answer

Verification: 'Are we building the product right?' (Conforms to specs). Validation: 'Are we building the right product?' (Meets user needs).

Explanation

You can verify a software perfectly against a spec document (0 bugs), but if the spec document doesn't solve the user's problem, validation fails.
